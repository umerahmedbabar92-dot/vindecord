FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a VIN?

A VIN is a unique vehicle identifier, normally 17 characters for modern vehicles.

Does VINDECORD automatically have every vehicle's full history?

No. History coverage depends on the authorized data provider and the records available for that vehicle.

Can I connect a paid vehicle-history API?

Yes. The production version should keep the provider API key on a server and retrieve the report through a secure backend.
